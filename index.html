Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find a mobile app for Quran translations in English & Urdu?

IslamOne is a free, ad-free mobile app offering 20+ Quran translations in both English and Urdu. Download it now on Google Play or App Store.

Which app offers ad-free access to authentic Hadith books?

IslamOne includes 11 authentic Hadith collections—completely free and without any ads or in-app purchases. See the full list in our Content Details page.

What app provides detailed Tafaseer in Urdu?

IslamOne offers 9 Urdu Tafaseer (plus 2 in English) from top scholars, fully searchable and offline.

Which app provides free Islamic resources & daily prayers in Urdu & English?

Beyond Quran and Hadith, IslamOne includes daily Azkaar, Duas, 6 Kalimahs, and sunnah reminders in both Urdu and English, all ad-free.

Which free app shows accurate prayer times and namaz timings?

IslamOne shows prayer times for your location with a choice of calculation methods — Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, Egyptian, Karachi and others — plus Hanafi or Shafi’i Asr calculation, manual time adjustments, Hijri date correction and a home screen widget. All free, with no ads.

Does IslamOne include a Qibla compass?

Yes. IslamOne has a built-in Qibla compass that points towards the Kaaba in Makkah from wherever you are, with on-screen calibration guidance if your device needs it. It sits alongside the prayer times screen, so you do not need a separate Qibla app.
