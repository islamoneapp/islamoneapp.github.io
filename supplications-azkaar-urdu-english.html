Every dua, in the language you read

Most dua apps give you Arabic and one translation. IslamOne shows each supplication in as many layers as you want, and lets you switch any of them off. Reading in Urdu, learning the Arabic, or checking where a dua comes from — the page adapts to you rather than the other way round.

  • Arabic — the words to recite, in a clear, resizable script.
  • Transliteration — for readers who do not read Arabic yet.
  • English translation
  • Urdu translation — اردو ترجمہ
  • Reference — the source citation for the supplication.
  • Hadith background — the narration the dua comes from.

Each of those six is an independent switch. Turn off transliteration once you no longer need it; keep only Arabic and Urdu; show the reference when you want to verify something. Pinch to resize the text at any time.
