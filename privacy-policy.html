Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

 Your privacy matters to us

IslamOne ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights — for both the IslamOne mobile app and the islamone.app website.

1. The App Collects No Personal Data

The IslamOne mobile app (iOS and Android) is designed to work fully offline. It does not:

  • Require account registration or sign-in
  • Collect your name, email, phone number, or location
  • Show ads or share data with ad networks
  • Make in-app purchases or charge any fees
  • Transmit any personal data to our servers

All Quran, Tafseer, Hadith, and other content is stored locally on your device after installation.

2. Website Analytics

The islamone.app website uses Vercel Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to collect anonymous, aggregated usage data. This may include:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Country-level location (not precise location)
  • Browser type and device category
  • Referral source (how you found the site)

This data is anonymous, never tied to an individual, and used solely to improve the website experience. Vercel's privacy practices are described at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

3. Third-Party App Stores

When you download IslamOne from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, those platforms may collect their own data (device identifiers, download records, crash reports) under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not control or receive this data.

4. Children's Privacy

IslamOne is suitable for all ages and is widely used by families. Because the app collects no personal data at all, there are no age-based restrictions or parental consent requirements within the app itself. App Store age ratings may still apply per platform guidelines.

5. Data Security

Since the app stores all content locally on your device and transmits no personal data, the primary security responsibility rests with your device's own security settings. For the website, Vercel uses industry-standard HTTPS encryption for all traffic.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal profile to access, correct, or delete. If you have questions about the anonymous analytics data collected by Vercel, you may contact Vercel directly. If you have any other privacy concerns, please contact us (see below).

7. Links to Other Websites

The IslamOne website and blog may contain links to external sites (e.g. Google Play, App Store, eQuran Library). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app or website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our social channels:

  • Facebook — IslamOneApp
  • Instagram — @islamoneapp
